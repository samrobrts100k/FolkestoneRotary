A Rotary lunch, minute by minute

Timings are a guide. Confirm with the club.

  1. 12:15

    Arrive and say hello

    Someone will meet you at the door and introduce you around.

  2. 12:30

    Lunch

    Sit wherever you like. Nobody keeps a seating plan.

  3. 12:50

    A speaker, or a cause

    Local people talk about what they do, from lifeboat crews to school heads.

  4. 1:20

    Club news and plans

    Who needs a hand this month. You can put your hand up, or just listen.
